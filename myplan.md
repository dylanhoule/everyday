# 28-Week Daily Plan: Calisthenics + First 50K (Finish + Get Faster)

*Beginner base (~10 mi/wk) built safely to a 50K finish, with a real speed progression layered on top.*

**Plan start:** Mon Jul 20, 2026  |  **Goal race (Week 28):** Jan 31, 2027

## How this plan works

- Priority #1 is staying uninjured. Nearly all running is **easy** (conversational). The single **hard session each week** is where speed comes from, so keep easy and hard clearly separated — jogging everything in one gray zone is exactly why 9:00 is your only gear.
- **5 run days + 3 strength days.** Tue = easy + strides. **Thu = the week's quality session** (tempo/intervals). Sun = long run. Sat = easy medium-long. Wed = easy.
- One hard quality day per week + strides twice a week. Speed and ultra-endurance pull in slightly different directions, but threshold work serves both, and as a beginner you'll gain on both at once.
- Every 4th week (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24) is a **cutback**: volume drops ~25-30% and quality becomes just easy + strides.
- Strength on **non-consecutive days (Mon/Wed/Fri)**. Leg day is Friday, ~48h before the Sunday long run.

## The four phases

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Long-run peak | Weekly peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 1-8 | Easy aerobic base; strides & hill sprints; first pull-up progressions | 10 mi | ~24 mi |
| Build 1 | 9-16 | Cruise intervals & tempo introduced; hills; back-to-backs | 16 mi | ~36 mi |
| Build 2 / Peak | 17-24 | VO2 + threshold quality; peak long runs; fast-finish long runs | 20 mi | ~48 mi |
| Taper / Race | 25-28 | Sharpen speed, cut volume, arrive fresh; 50K on Week 28 | 31 mi (race) | taper |

## What makes you faster here

- **Threshold / tempo work** (the backbone): raises the pace you hold before lactate piles up, so easy pace drops.
- **Strides 2x/week + hill sprints:** cheap neuromuscular speed and economy, almost no fatigue cost.
- **VO2 intervals** in the peak block: lift the top end so threshold feels easier underneath it.
- **Fast-finish long runs:** running quick on tired legs — the skill behind both a fast 5K and a strong 50K.
- **Time trials in Weeks 2, 13, 26** set your paces and prove the speed is arriving.

## Safety rules baked in

- **Long-run cap:** no long run jumps >~10% above your longest run of the past 30 days.
- **Strength training** is the most evidence-backed injury-prevention tool for runners (~halves overuse injuries).
- **Only one hard day a week** during the ramp. If legs are cooked, drop the quality day, never the long run.
- **Nothing hard on race week.** Gait-altering or sharp one-sided pain = stop and rest.

## Paces & benchmarks

*Estimates anchored to a ~9:00 easy pace. Run the Week 2 time trial and reset off your real numbers.*

| Zone | Effort (RPE) | Feel | Est. pace (min/mi) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery | 3-4 | Fully conversational, feels almost too slow | 9:45 - 10:45 |
| Easy / aerobic | 4-5 | Talk in full sentences; nose-breathing | 8:45 - 9:30 |
| Marathon / ultra effort | 6 | Comfortable-moderate; short sentences only | 8:10 - 8:40 |
| Threshold / tempo | 7-8 | 'Comfortably hard'; ~1-hour race effort; rhythmic breathing | 7:50 - 8:15 |
| VO2 / 5K | 9 | Hard; breathing labored; sustainable ~3-5 min | 7:10 - 7:40 |
| Strides | - | Smooth fast turnover, NOT a sprint; full recovery | ~6:50 - 7:20 feel |
| Hill sprints | - | 10-20s near-max uphill; full walk-down recovery | by effort |

**From your 1-mile time-trial (TT) pace:** threshold ≈ mile pace + 45-60 s/mi · easy ≈ mile pace + 2:00-2:30/mi · VO2/5K ≈ mile pace + 15-25 s/mi.

| When | Test | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Week 2 | 1-mile time trial | After an easy warm-up, run 1 mile as a hard, even effort. This is your baseline — set every pace zone from it. |
| Week 13 | 1-mile (or 2-mile) time trial | Repeat the test. Expect a faster time; recalibrate your zones. This is your mid-plan speed check. |
| Week 26 | 5K time trial or local parkrun | Gauge race fitness before the final taper sharpening. A big drop from Week 2 is your proof it's working. |

*Time-trial how-to: easy 10-min warm-up + a few strides, then run the distance as a hard, EVEN effort. Note the time and rebuild your zones.*

## Calisthenics progression (beginner, minimal gear)

*3 sessions/week (Mon/Wed/Fri). Advance only when all 3 sets feel doable with ~2 reps left. Warm up 5 min first.*

| Block | Weeks | Mon — Push+Core | Wed — Pull+Core | Fri — Legs (eccentric)+Core | Core |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-4 | Incline push-ups (hands on table) 3x8-12; Chair/bench dips 3x8 | Inverted rows under a sturdy table, feet forward 3x8; Dead hang 3x20s | Bodyweight squats 3x15; Eccentric calf raises (3s down) 3x12; Glute bridges 3x15 | Plank 3x30s; Bird-dog 3x8/side; Hollow hold 3x20s |
| 2 | 5-8 | Knee-to-full push-ups 3x8-10; Bench dips 3x10 | Inverted rows, lower angle 3x10; Scapular pulls 3x8; Dead hang 3x30s | Split squats 3x10/leg; Eccentric calf raises 3x15; Single-leg glute bridge 3x10/leg | Plank 3x45s; Side plank 3x20s/side; Leg raises 3x12 |
| 3 | 9-12 | Full push-ups 3x8-12; Chair dips 3x10-12 | Australian pull-ups (bar/rings ~hip height) 3x8-10; Negative pull-ups (5s lower) 3x3 | Bulgarian split squats 3x8/leg; Single-leg calf raises 3x12; Nordic hamstring negatives 3x4 | Plank 3x60s; Side plank 3x30s/side; Hollow hold 3x30s |
| 4 | 13-16 | Full push-ups 3x12-15; Pike push-ups 3x6-8 | Australian pull-ups 3x10-12; Negative pull-ups (5s) 3x4 | Bulgarian split squats 3x10/leg; Step-downs 3x8/leg; Nordic negatives 3x5 | Hanging knee raises 3x8; Side plank w/ reach 3x30s; Superman 3x12 |
| 5 | 17-20 | Decline / close-grip push-ups 3x8-12; Pike push-ups 3x8-10 | Negative pull-ups (5s) 3x5; Band- or foot-assisted pull-ups 3x5 | Box pistol squats 3x5/leg; Single-leg calf raises 3x15; Nordic negatives 3x6 | Hanging leg raises 3x8; Hollow rock 3x15; Side plank 3x40s/side |
| 6 | 21-24 | Archer / deficit push-ups 3x6-8; Straight-bar or parallel-bar dips 3x6-8 | Full pull-ups (as able) 3x AMRAP; Chin-ups 3x3-5 | Assisted pistol squats 3x6/leg; Low box jumps 3x8; Nordic negatives 3x6 | Hanging leg raises 3x10; Hollow rock 3x20; L-sit tuck holds 3x15s |
| 7 | 25-28 | MAINTAIN: push-ups & dips 2x8-10 (leave reps in reserve) | MAINTAIN: pull-ups/rows 2x, submaximal. No max efforts race week. | MAINTAIN: light squats + eccentric calf raises 2x12. NO plyo/Nordics race week. | Light plank & hollow holds 2x. |

**Why:** push/pull balanced 1:1 to protect shoulders; legs eccentric-focused for tendon durability and more force per stride; core holds your stride together late in long runs; progress by harder leverage, not endless reps.
