# 28-Week Daily Plan: Calisthenics + First 50K

*Built for a beginner base (~10 mi/wk, 3-5 mi long run) building safely to a 50K finish.*

**Plan start:** Mon Jul 20, 2026  |  **Goal race (Week 28):** Jan 31, 2027

## How this plan works

- Priority #1 is staying uninjured. The fastest way to fail a first 50K is ramping volume too fast, so nearly all running is **easy** (conversational pace). If you can't talk, slow down.
- **5 run days + 3 short strength (calisthenics) days** per week. The Sunday long run is the engine of the plan; protect it and build it patiently.
- Every 4th week (Weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24) is a **cutback/deload** week: volume drops ~25-30% so your body absorbs the work and rebuilds.
- Strength sits on **non-consecutive days (Mon/Wed/Fri)** so it doesn't compete with key runs. Leg day is Friday, ~48h before the Sunday long run.
- Advance a calisthenics exercise only when all 3 sets feel doable with **~2 reps left in the tank**. Never grind to failure with a long run coming up.

## The four phases

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Long-run peak | Weekly peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | 1-8 | Easy aerobic volume; movement foundation; first pull-up progressions | 10 mi | ~24 mi |
| Build 1 | 9-16 | Longer long runs; hills; back-to-back weekends begin | 16 mi | ~36 mi |
| Build 2 / Peak | 17-24 | Peak long runs + big back-to-back weekends; time on feet | 20 mi | ~48 mi |
| Taper / Race | 25-28 | Cut volume, keep sharpness, arrive fresh; 50K on Week 28 | 31 mi (race) | taper |

## Evidence-based rules baked in

- **Long-run cap:** no long run jumps more than ~10% above your longest run of the past 30 days (spikes of 30%+ sharply raise injury risk).
- **Strength training is the single most evidence-backed injury-prevention tool** for runners (roughly halves overuse injuries) — calisthenics is non-negotiable, not optional.
- **Eccentric loading** (slow calf raises, Nordic hamstring negatives, tempo squats) builds tendon/tissue tolerance for ultra volume.
- **Concurrent strength + endurance on non-consecutive days** preserves gains from both and limits interference.
- The **50K itself will be longer than your longest training run** (normal for ultras): time-on-feet, walking hills, and fueling cover the gap.

## Gear, fueling & pacing notes

- One worthwhile purchase: a cheap **doorway pull-up bar** (or use a low playground bar / sturdy table for rows). Everything else is bodyweight.
- **Easy pace = full sentences.** Long runs should feel almost too slow. Walk the uphills on long runs from Week 9 on — that's a skill, not cheating.
- **Practice fueling** on any run over ~90 min: ~200-300 cal/hr (gels, chews, real food) and sip fluids. Test everything in training; nothing new on race day.
- This plan gives you **two true rest days (Mon/Fri)**. If you want to keep a run streak, an optional very-easy 1-mile walk/jog is fine, but real rest is what prevents injury.
- Aches that change your gait, or sharp/one-sided pain, mean **stop and rest**. Repeat a week rather than push through — an injury costs months.

## Calisthenics progression (beginner, minimal gear)

*3 sessions/week (Mon/Wed/Fri). Advance to the next block only when all 3 sets feel doable with ~2 reps left. Warm up 5 min first.*

| Block | Weeks | Mon — Push+Core | Wed — Pull+Core | Fri — Legs (eccentric)+Core | Core (rotate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-4 | Incline push-ups (hands on table) 3x8-12; Chair/bench dips 3x8 | Inverted rows under a sturdy table, feet forward 3x8; Dead hang 3x20s | Bodyweight squats 3x15; Eccentric calf raises (3s down) 3x12; Glute bridges 3x15 | Plank 3x30s; Bird-dog 3x8/side; Hollow hold 3x20s |
| 2 | 5-8 | Knee-to-full push-ups 3x8-10; Bench dips 3x10 | Inverted rows, lower angle 3x10; Scapular pulls 3x8; Dead hang 3x30s | Split squats 3x10/leg; Eccentric calf raises 3x15; Single-leg glute bridge 3x10/leg | Plank 3x45s; Side plank 3x20s/side; Leg raises 3x12 |
| 3 | 9-12 | Full push-ups 3x8-12; Chair dips 3x10-12 | Australian pull-ups (bar/rings ~hip height) 3x8-10; Negative pull-ups (5s lower) 3x3 | Bulgarian split squats 3x8/leg; Single-leg calf raises 3x12; Nordic hamstring negatives 3x4 | Plank 3x60s; Side plank 3x30s/side; Hollow hold 3x30s |
| 4 | 13-16 | Full push-ups 3x12-15; Pike push-ups 3x6-8 | Australian pull-ups 3x10-12; Negative pull-ups (5s) 3x4 | Bulgarian split squats 3x10/leg; Step-downs 3x8/leg; Nordic negatives 3x5 | Hanging knee raises 3x8; Side plank w/ reach 3x30s; Superman 3x12 |
| 5 | 17-20 | Decline / close-grip push-ups 3x8-12; Pike push-ups 3x8-10 | Negative pull-ups (5s) 3x5; Band- or foot-assisted pull-ups 3x5 | Box pistol squats 3x5/leg; Single-leg calf raises 3x15; Nordic negatives 3x6 | Hanging leg raises 3x8; Hollow rock 3x15; Side plank 3x40s/side |
| 6 | 21-24 | Archer / deficit push-ups 3x6-8; Straight-bar or parallel-bar dips 3x6-8 | Full pull-ups (as able) 3x AMRAP; Chin-ups 3x3-5 | Assisted pistol squats 3x6/leg; Low box jumps 3x8; Nordic negatives 3x6 | Hanging leg raises 3x10; Hollow rock 3x20; L-sit tuck holds 3x15s |
| 7 | 25-28 | MAINTAIN: push-ups & dips 2x8-10 (leave reps in reserve) | MAINTAIN: pull-ups/rows 2x, submaximal. No max efforts race week. | MAINTAIN: light squats + eccentric calf raises 2x12. NO plyo/Nordics race week. | Light plank & hollow holds 2x. |

**Why these movements:** push and pull are balanced 1:1 to protect the shoulders; legs are eccentric-focused to build the tendon durability ultra mileage demands; core keeps your stride intact when you fatigue late in long runs; progress by harder leverage (incline → full → decline → archer), not endless reps.
