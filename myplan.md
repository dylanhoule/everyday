## Read this part once, then live in the daily list

### The honest situation

You'll be a rising sophomore in summer 2027. That is the hardest internship market in CS. Most postings gate on graduation year in a way that excludes you, the named freshman programs have shrunk hard since 2023 (Meta University is confirmed shut down, Google STEP's status is unresolved, Code2040 closed permanently in May 2026), and you're competing against juniors who have two more years of coursework.

Two things work in your favor and they are not small. You already do freelance web dev, which means you can point at things you built for people who paid you — most freshmen cannot. And Georgia Tech CS is a top-tier recruiting pipeline with employers physically on campus in week 4.

The realistic read: a paid Bay Area SWE internship after freshman year is achievable but uncommon. Plan for it, execute hard, and understand that the more likely good outcomes are a smaller-company or startup role, paid research, or continued freelance — any of which sets you up to win the sophomore cycle, which starts around August 2027.

### The strategy, in order of expected value

1. **Y Combinator startups and small Bay Area companies.** They never ask your class year, they're concentrated in the Bay Area, and they hire on demonstrated ability. Your freelance TypeScript work is directly relevant. This is your highest-probability path and most freshmen ignore it.
2. **Ungated big-company reqs.** Many postings say "currently enrolled in a bachelor's program" with no class-year floor. Pinterest, Stripe, Coinbase, DoorDash, Cloudflare, Intuit, Apple, Google's SWE Intern BS role, and SpaceX (whose reqs publish a freshman/sophomore pay rate outright). Volume game — apply to all of them.
3. **Explore Microsoft.** The only major program with an explicit first-year track. Has an Atlanta location. Rolling applications, opens Aug–Oct, fills fast. Check weekly.
4. **NVIDIA Ignite.** Freshman/sophomore pre-internship in Santa Clara. Bay Area. No published dates — monitor the university recruiting page.
5. **GT undergraduate research.** PURA pays $2,000, has no class-year restriction, and is the least competitive real opportunity available to you. Requires a faculty mentor, which requires cold emails in October.
6. **The specialist programs.** Break Through Tech AI (states "first, second, or third year"), Jane Street JSIP (first- and second-years, $12,500), Contrary Venture Partners (documented freshman placements). Lottery tickets with cheap tickets.

### What does not work

Waiting for postings that say "freshman." Across 14,703 tracked listings last cycle, zero contained the word. Class year is an invisible filter, not a written one. You apply to ungated reqs and let them screen you out — you don't screen yourself out first.

### Two things I could not confirm

- **You said classes start Aug 20.** GT's registrar says the first day of classes is **Monday, Aug 24, 2026**. Aug 20 is probably FASET or move-in. The plan uses Aug 24; adjust if you know better.
- **Bloomberg** shows up on every "best freshman internship" list, but its req reads "have completed at least one year of undergraduate studies **at time of application**." Applying in September 2026, you'll have completed zero. It's likely out for Summer 2027. Apply anyway if a window opens in late spring, but don't build around it.

## How to use this file

Every day has two versions:

- **Main** — what to do on a normal day. Usually 45–90 minutes.
- **20-min** — the floor. You said a bad week means 20 minutes. This is the version for those days.

**The floor is the actual plan.** Doing the 20-minute version 300 times beats doing the full version 40 times and then quitting in November. If you're choosing between skipping and doing the floor, do the floor. If you're choosing between the floor and beating yourself up about not doing more, do the floor and move on.

Missing a day is fine. Missing a week is fine. Just start again at today's date rather than trying to catch up — catch-up debt is what kills these.
